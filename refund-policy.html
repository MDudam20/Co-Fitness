Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

This Refund Policy governs all purchases made through Coach Dhananjay (CD Fitness). By purchasing any service from our website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund Policy.

1. Nature of Our Services

Coach Dhananjay provides personalized digital fitness services, including but not limited to AI-generated diet plans, workout programs, nutrition guides, fitness reports, and downloadable digital content.

Since all products are digital in nature and are generated specifically for each customer, they cannot be physically returned once delivered.

2. General Refund Policy

All purchases made through our website are considered final.

Once a digital service has been generated, delivered, downloaded, viewed, accessed, or made available to the customer, it becomes non-refundable.

No cancellation, refund, exchange, replacement, credit, or partial refund shall be available except in the limited situations specifically described below.

3. Refunds Will Not Be Granted For

Refund requests shall not be accepted for reasons including, but not limited to:

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Accidental purchase of a plan.
  • Purchase of the wrong package or membership.
  • Dissatisfaction with food choices.
  • Dissatisfaction with workout recommendations.
  • Failure to follow the recommended plan.
  • Lack of motivation.
  • Personal scheduling issues.
  • Travel or relocation.
  • Failure to achieve desired results.
  • Subjective dissatisfaction with plan design or presentation.
  • Availability of similar content elsewhere.
  • Failure to read the service description before purchasing.

4. Limited Refund Exceptions

Coach Dhananjay may, at its sole discretion, consider a refund only in the following situations:

  • Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • Successful payment but complete failure of digital delivery due to a verified technical issue.
  • Purchased digital service cannot be generated because of an internal system error that cannot be resolved within a reasonable period.

Eligibility for any refund shall always be determined solely by Coach Dhananjay after verification of the claim.

5. Refund Request Process

To request a refund under an eligible circumstance, the customer must:

  • Submit the refund request within seven (7) days of the transaction.
  • Provide complete transaction details.
  • Provide payment proof if requested.
  • Cooperate during verification.

Coach Dhananjay reserves the right to reject any incomplete, fraudulent, misleading, or unverifiable refund request.

6. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the refund shall be processed only through the original payment method used for the transaction.

Refund processing time depends upon the respective payment gateway, bank, credit card issuer, or financial institution.

Coach Dhananjay shall not be responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers.

7. Digital Products

Customers acknowledge that digital products cannot be returned once delivered because the information contained within them is permanently accessible after delivery.

For this reason, refunds are generally unavailable after successful digital delivery.

8. Fraudulent Claims

Coach Dhananjay reserves the right to refuse any refund request that appears fraudulent, abusive, repetitive, misleading, or intended to misuse this policy.

Accounts involved in fraudulent refund activities may be suspended or permanently terminated without prior notice.

9. Policy Updates

Coach Dhananjay reserves the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time. Updated versions shall become effective immediately upon publication on this website.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Refund Policy, please contact us through the official contact details available on our website.
