Your personal information may be used to:

Certain services require information relating to your fitness, nutrition, lifestyle, allergies, injuries, medications, and medical history. Such information is collected solely for generating personalized fitness recommendations and digital plans. 4. Payment Information

Payments are securely processed through trusted third-party payment gateways.

CD Fitness does not collect or store your complete credit card number, debit card number, CVV, PIN, internet banking password, or similar payment credentials. Payment information is securely handled by authorized payment service providers.

CD Fitness implements reasonable administrative, technical, organizational, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain customer records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, detect fraud, and enforce our agreements.
