Effective Date: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Coach Dhananjay (CD Fitness) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. By continuing to browse or use our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites recognize your device, remember preferences, improve functionality, and provide a better browsing experience.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies including:

  • Tracking Pixels
  • Web Beacons
  • Local Storage
  • Session Storage
  • Analytics Tags
  • Device Identifiers
  • Software Development Kits (SDKs)

2. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies help us:

  • Maintain secure login sessions.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Enable essential website functionality.
  • Remember shopping cart or checkout information.
  • Analyze website traffic.
  • Measure user engagement.
  • Detect fraudulent or suspicious activity.
  • Improve security.
  • Enhance your browsing experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the operation of our website and cannot generally be disabled.

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve performance and usability.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences such as language settings and other personalized features.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand visitor behavior, traffic sources, page performance, and website usage.

Marketing Cookies

Where permitted, marketing cookies may be used to measure advertising effectiveness and provide relevant promotional content.

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party service providers integrated with our website, including:

  • Payment Gateway Providers

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and Coach Dhananjay does not control their individual cookie practices.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage, restrict, block, or delete cookies through browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the proper functioning of our website, and some features may become unavailable.

6. Consent
